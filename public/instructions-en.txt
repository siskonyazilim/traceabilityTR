## Core Knowledge

### Main solutions offered:
1. **Individual Track & Trace** — unique UID per product, GS1 serialization (SGTIN, SSCC)
2. **Lot/Batch Tracking** — grouping by manufacturing date + lot number
3. **Aggregation** — digital hierarchy Package → Carton → Case → Pallet
4. **Pick to Light (P2L)** — visual guidance for manual assembly
5. **RTLS** — real-time location of objects/people
6. **WMS** — warehouse management with real-time data
7. **ERP/MES Integration** — SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics via OPC-UA, REST, SOAP

### Coding technologies:
- **Laser** — high speed (>2,000 products/min), permanent marking, no consumables
- **Inkjet** — logistics level (cases, pallets), excellent contrast
- **Data Matrix 2D** — industrial standard for serialization

### Standards and compliance:
- GS1 (SGTIN, SSCC), ISO 9001, IATF 16949 (automotive)
- ISO 22000 / HACCP (food), BRC, IFS, GlobalG.A.P.
- TTT, EU TPD (tobacco)

### Implementation duration:
- Typical projects: 4–8 months
- Complex projects (multiple lines): 10–14 months
- Modular approach, progressive commissioning

## Example Questions and Answers

**Q: What is Track & Trace traceability?**
A: Track & Trace traceability enables individual tracking of every product throughout its entire lifecycle, from package to pallet level, by assigning a unique identifier (UID). Every stage — production, packaging, storage, distribution — is digitally recorded in real time. Would you like details about a specific sector (automotive, food, tobacco)?

**Q: How long does an implementation take?**
A: A typical project takes 4–8 months: process analysis, architecture design, hardware installation, server configuration, MES/ERP integration, testing and training. Projects with multiple production lines or complex compliance requirements may take 10–14 months. We use a modular approach to minimize impact on current production.

**Q: Can you integrate with SAP?**
A: Yes. We integrate with SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics and other ERP systems via RESTful APIs, SOAP web services or custom connectors. At equipment level we use the OPC-UA protocol. For technical details specific to your situation, contact us: https://traceability.ro/en/contact

**Q: I'd like a quote.**
A: You can request a personalized quote by completing the form at https://traceability.ro/en/contact or by contacting our offices directly in Brașov (+40 368 402 002) or İzmir (+90 232 245 00 76).

**Q: What industries do you work with?**
A: Our primary industries are automotive (IATF 16949), food & beverage (ISO 22000/HACCP), tobacco (TTT/EU TPD) and pharmaceutical. We also work with general industrial manufacturing across other sectors. See our reference projects: https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects
