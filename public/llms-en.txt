# Traceability.ro

> Traceability.ro is an industrial automation company delivering innovative traceability solutions for smart factories and sustainable manufacturing. Operated by Siskon Endüstriyel Otomasyon Sistemleri A.Ş., with offices in Romania (Brașov) and Turkey (İzmir).

Traceability.ro delivers industrial traceability, MES and automation solutions for smart factories: RFID, RTLS, WMS, Poka Yoke and end-to-end integration.

## Solutions & Products

- Individual Product Tracking (Track & Trace with unique UID per item)
- Lot/Batch Tracking (grouped by production date and batch number)
- Pick to Light (P2L — visual guidance for manual assembly operations)
- RTLS — Real Time Location Systems (real-time tracking of objects and people)
- WMS — Warehouse Management Systems
- ERP/MES Integration (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics — OPC-UA, REST, SOAP protocols)

## Industries Served

## Reference Projects

- [Maxion İnci Çelik — Pallet Traceability](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/maxion-inci-celik): Automotive — communication software between Beckhoff PLC and pallet applicator, automatic 2D code printing by recipe.
- [Abalıoğlu Yağ — Traceability](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/abalioglu-yag): Food — pallet traceability and automatic carton labelling for one of Turkey's largest oil producers.
- [Nuh'un Ankara — Traceability](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/nuhun-ankara): Food — integrated carton, pallet and transport traceability.
- [Delphi Technologies — Warehouse Management](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/delphi-technologies): Automotive — integrated WMS with full visibility from production output to shipment.
- [PMI — RFID for Stamping](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/pmi-rfid): Tobacco — SAP integration ensuring correct stamping unit per production order.
- [Delphi Technologies — Individual Product Monitoring (Injection Rail Assembly)](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/delphi-individual-monitoring-injection-rail): Automotive.
- [Mey Diageo — Camera Label Inspection System](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/mey-diageo-camera-label-control): Food — camera system inspecting bottle labels during packaging.
- [PMI — Filter Tracking](https://traceability.ro/en/reference-projects/pmi-filter-tracking): Tobacco — 50,300 RFID tags, 109 fixed read/write heads, full cigarette filter traceability across production area.
